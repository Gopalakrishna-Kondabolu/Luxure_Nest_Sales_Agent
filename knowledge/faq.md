# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I book a private consultation?
A: You can book directly through WhatsApp by telling us your preferred date and time, or by asking our AI assistant to check open slots.

Q: Can I view off-market properties without a consultation?
A: Initial non-disclosure verification is required for off-market listings. A short 15-minute discovery call is recommended first.

Q: What locations do you cover?
A: Luxure Nest specializes in prime luxury coastal estates, metropolis penthouses, and private countryside retreats.

Q: Can I reschedule or cancel my booking?
A: Yes, bookings can be cancelled or rescheduled up to 24 hours in advance through WhatsApp or by contacting concierge desk.
