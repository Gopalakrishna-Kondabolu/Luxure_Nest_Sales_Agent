# Company Policies

1. **Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy**
   - Bookings must be cancelled or modified at least 24 hours prior to the appointment.
   - Initial bookings are created with status `PENDING` until validated by backend / concierge team.

2. **Client Confidentiality & Privacy**
   - All client communications, financial requirements, and property inquiries are protected under strict confidentiality. Client details are never shared with third parties without express consent.

3. **Information Accuracy Policy**
   - The AI Assistant relies exclusively on verified Luxure Nest knowledge base files. If exact information is unavailable, the assistant will inform the client politely and offer human specialist escalation.
