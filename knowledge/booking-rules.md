# Booking & Appointment Rules

1. **Advance Notice**: Appointments must be requested at least 24 hours in advance.
2. **Time Slots**: Consultations are scheduled on the hour (e.g., 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 2:00 PM, 4:00 PM EST).
3. **Information Required for Booking**:
   - Client Name
   - Service Interest (e.g. Property Matchmaking, Interior Staging, VIP Consultation)
   - Preferred Date & Time
   - Phone Number (WhatsApp ID)
4. **Booking Status**: All new bookings are initially created with status `PENDING`.
5. **Confirmation**: Once availability is checked and details are registered, the assistant presents the `PENDING` booking confirmation details to the client.
